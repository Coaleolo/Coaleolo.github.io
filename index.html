About

I'm a 2D artist and game developer who also writes the code behind what I draw. I design characters from concept through layered, rig-ready art, then bring them into Unity and connect them to local AI systems.

I've led art direction on team games, built an accessible custom controller, redesigned an interactive narrative app, and shipped a company website as an intern. I enjoy working between artists and engineers.

Outside of class I run creative workshops and peer critiques as president of the Anime Club, and I've managed budgets for a student club.
